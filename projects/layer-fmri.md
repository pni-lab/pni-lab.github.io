## Layer-fMRI and predictive processing

How does cortex implement predictive processing — separating **bottom-up** sensory drive from **top-down** cognitive modulation? We use submillimeter **7T laminar fMRI** to test layer-specific computations in human pain processing.

### Key study

[Kincses et al. — *Layer-specific cortical processing dissociates sensory and cognitive influences on pain*](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.12.724526v1)

Using a factorial manipulation of nociceptive input and cognitive (distraction) modulation, bottom-up processing was strongest in **middle** cortical layers, whereas top-down modulation preferentially engaged **superficial** layers — consistent with cortico-cortical feedback. Individual differences in this laminar segregation predicted distraction-induced analgesia, linking circuit architecture to behaviour.
