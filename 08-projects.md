:header: **ERA-NET NEUROFLEX**
International consortium on cognitive flexibility and large-scale brain dynamics as targets for chronic pain.

:::{grid-item-card}
:link: ./projects/attractor-networks.md
:header: **Large-scale brain attractor networks**
A scale-free, first-principles computational framework linking connectivity to activity across levels of description.
:::

:::{grid-item-card}
:link: ./projects/predictive-models.md
:header: **Predictive models**
MRI-based brain signatures of behaviour and clinical traits, plus methods that make such models more robust and replicable.
:::

:::{grid-item-card}
:link: ./projects/layer-fmri.md
:header: **Layer-fMRI and predictive processing**
7T laminar fMRI to ask how cortical layers implement bottom-up sensory drive and top-down cognitive modulation.
:::

:::{grid-item-card}
:link: ./projects/placebo-imaging.md
:header: **Placebo Imaging Consortium**
International consortium meta-analysing individual-participant fMRI of placebo analgesia.
:::

:::{grid-item-card}
:link: ./projects/rlbf.md
:header: **RLBF — Reinforcement Learning via Brain Feedback**
Closed-loop real-time fMRI in which reinforcement learning adapts stimuli from ongoing neural responses.
:::

:::{grid-item-card}

:header: **Scientific discourse & good practices**
Comments, tools and papers on replicability, open science, and trustworthy predictive neuroimaging.
